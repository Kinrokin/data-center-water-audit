## Data Center Water & Infrastructure Audit

Reconstructed a widely circulated 228-billion-gallon U.S. data-center water claim from primary-source inputs; separated direct facility consumption from electricity-related attribution; audited water-accounting boundaries, local utility capacity, cooling tradeoffs, large-load financial exposure, AI-workload utility, and verification claims; published reproducible calculations, a machine-readable source register, project-level audit schema, and public-record request templates.

**Demonstrated skills:** evidence synthesis, reproducible analysis, systems thinking, adversarial review, policy/technical boundary analysis, data schema design, Python, public documentation.
