## What is established

The public record contains a pre-September financial crisis, an independent audit with a segregation-of-duties deficiency, a City CDBG processing chronology, a signed lease and authorizing ordinance, competing accounts of the employment decision, and a September 28 tentative YMCA operating proposal.

The contribution/grant category decreased $512,652 between FY2024 and FY2025. The largest component is a $321,135 decrease in a combined governmental-grants category; the specific awards are still unidentified. Cash, accrued revenue and state vendor disbursements are not interchangeable.

## What is not established

An authenticated funding-for-departure condition, its original speaker, a completed September operator handoff, a permanent successor, an unlawful asset transfer, or the political-message commissioner. MAP figures remain provisional. Full IRS Form 990 PDF/XML downloads failed; the included financial audit is **not** a tax return. A public board resolution is not filed Articles of Dissolution or an AG receipt.
