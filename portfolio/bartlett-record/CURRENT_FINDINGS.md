[P1 p6; adversarial audit]

## Property and rent: actual instruments, not generic assumptions

Special Ordinance 10608 and the signed lease are the starting point. Bartlett's premises exclude Rooms 8-11. The lease describes a family-resource program broader than exclusive childcare use of the entire building and has distinct rental, assignment, subletting, amendment, termination, insurance and indemnity provisions. A trade-fixture retention clause is conditional, not a transfer of every Bartlett asset. [P1 pp8-18]

Candidate rent arithmetic: seven months at a reconstructed $2,806.88 less four payments of $4,912.03 leaves $0.04. Adding that to three $2,905.12 months reproduces $8,715.40. The lease's printed monthly and annual figures contain discrepancies, so this is a candidate explanation, not an authenticated receivable ledger or a legal conclusion about billing. [P1 p12; P2]

The City-posted emergency procurement provision does not contain a 120-day cap. Whether a purchasing route applies depends on the actual transaction and public expenditure. A lease, limited entry, management service and grant are not automatically the same transaction. The posted policy is not represented as a certified complete current-code compilation. [P5]

## The personnel allegation remains a source-and-transmission question

Williams publicly described a message that help or money would not be available while she remained. The original speaker, messenger, resource, exact condition and claimed authority remain unidentified. The City denies requesting resignation or exchanging rent relief for removal. The board describes acceptance of a resignation, which Williams disputes. Declining transition assistance is not automatically the same as requiring an existing employee's departure as a condition of assistance. [P2; N1]

The appropriate voluntary first question is to ask Williams to describe the discussion in her own sequence, then clarify whom 'we' included, who spoke, whether she heard it directly or through someone else, and which assistance was discussed. Do not suggest a suspect. Separate firsthand words, paraphrase, inference and later-acquired knowledge. Records requests and witness protocols in the full dossier remain unsent/unconducted unless separately documented.

## Closure standard

Identify the actor, resource, authority, condition, action and consequence. Keep offer, approval, signature, publication, effectiveness and actual operation separate. A lawful transition instrument can coexist with an unresolved personnel allegation; an administrative discrepancy can exist without a coordinated removal. Findings must change when the evidence changes, not when a more compelling narrative is proposed.
