# Public-record request template: electric utility / regulator

Subject: Records concerning large-load interconnection, capacity, and cost allocation

Under the applicable public-records law, I request electronic copies of records concerning [PROJECT / LARGE-LOAD CUSTOMER] for the period [DATE RANGE].

Please include, where maintained and publicly releasable:

- load letters, interconnection or service requests, requested MW, ramp schedules, and expected load factor;
- system-impact, transmission, distribution, generation, reliability, and resource-adequacy studies;
- executed tariffs, special contracts, minimum-demand provisions, take-or-pay provisions, deposits, collateral, guarantees, or other financial-security terms;
- estimates and final costs for project-driven generation, transmission, distribution, substation, or other infrastructure;
- analysis of which costs are project-specific versus shared system benefits;
- curtailment, flexible-load, staged-connection, or operating-limit provisions;
- records describing treatment of the obligation after delay, downsizing, cancellation, sale, or assignment;
- staff memoranda, testimony, orders, or presentations supporting approval or cost allocation.

I am not requesting material that must be withheld for grid security or other lawful reasons. Please segregate and release non-exempt portions and identify the legal basis for any withholding.
