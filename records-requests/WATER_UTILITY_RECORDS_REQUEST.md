# Public-record request template: water utility / municipality

Subject: Records concerning large-load water service and data-center demand

Under the applicable public-records law, I request electronic copies of records concerning proposed, approved, or operating data-center or comparable large-load water customers for the period [DATE RANGE].

Please include, where maintained:

- service applications, demand forecasts, engineering studies, and capacity analyses;
- expected annual, peak-day, and peak-hour water demand;
- identified source water, backup source, reclaimed-water arrangements, and drought or emergency operating plans;
- treatment, pumping, storage, and conveyance capacity studies relevant to the project;
- executed service agreements, development agreements, cost-sharing agreements, guarantees, minimum-payment provisions, and amendments;
- records showing other committed large-load demand included in the same capacity analysis;
- monitoring reports or operating data for water delivery, return flow, and consumptive use where available;
- staff memoranda or presentations used to support an approval, denial, restriction, or staged-service decision.

I am not requesting protected personal information or information that the law requires the agency to withhold. If a record contains both releasable and exempt material, please provide the releasable portion and identify the legal basis for each redaction or withholding.

Please provide records in their native electronic format when reasonably available, including spreadsheets and machine-readable data rather than print-to-PDF versions.
