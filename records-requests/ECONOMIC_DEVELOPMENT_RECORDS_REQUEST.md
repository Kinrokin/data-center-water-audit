# Public-record request template: economic development / incentives

Subject: Records concerning public incentives and infrastructure commitments for [PROJECT]

Under the applicable public-records law, I request electronic copies of records concerning public incentives, abatements, infrastructure commitments, and fiscal projections associated with [PROJECT] for the period [DATE RANGE].

Please include, where maintained:

- incentive applications and executed agreements;
- tax abatements, exemptions, credits, grants, land transfers, public financing, or infrastructure commitments;
- projected and actual construction employment, permanent employment, payroll, taxable value, and public revenue;
- fiscal-impact or cost-benefit analyses and the assumptions used;
- clawback, performance, reporting, default, sale, assignment, and termination provisions;
- payments or contributions actually received from the developer;
- public costs for roads, utilities, emergency services, or other project-specific support;
- staff memoranda or presentations used in the approval process.

Please provide underlying spreadsheets in native format where reasonably available.
