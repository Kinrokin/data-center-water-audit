I started digging into the 228-billion-gallon data-center claim because I thought the number was being used without enough context. Some of the first answers I accepted were wrong. That is part of the article too.

The number itself is legitimate under the cited Berkeley Lab model. It does not mean 228 billion gallons flowed through server buildings, and it does not tell you whether a particular town has enough water for the next project. Those are different questions, and I followed both of them as far as I could.

I put the full article, source ledger, calculations, and reproduction script in one public repository so nobody has to take my word for any of it:
