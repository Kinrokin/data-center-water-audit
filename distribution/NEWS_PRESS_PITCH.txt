Subject: Source-backed audit of the 228B-gallon data-center water claim

I started with a simple question: is the viral 228-billion-gallon figure real, and what does it actually tell a local community?

I reconstructed the national estimate from Berkeley Lab’s reported inputs and then separated that footprint from the evidence needed to judge a specific project: source water, peak utility capacity, cooling design, backup supply, grid demand, cost allocation, and enforceable commitments.

I am a local nurse who also works on AI systems. The complete package is public and reproducible, including the article, source ledger, calculation script, and records-request templates:

https://github.com/Kinrokin/data-center-water-audit
