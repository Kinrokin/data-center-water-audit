# Limitations

- The headline 228-billion-gallon result is a reconstruction of a published model, not a national census of facility meters.
- The reconstructed total is not an estimate of generative AI alone.
- Average electricity-water attribution does not by itself identify the causal water impact of a new load.
- National comparisons do not determine local source adequacy.
- Utility water delivery is not automatically equal to consumptive loss.
- The repository does not claim a complete lifecycle assessment for the data-center industry.
- Productivity and scientific-capability studies are evidence about their studied tasks and populations; they are not universal benefit multipliers.
- A useful AI application does not automatically create an environmental offset for unrelated workloads.
- The policy framework is a proposed decision method, not a statement that every jurisdiction currently has the same authority or rules.
- Forecast uncertainty cannot be eliminated. The framework therefore favors explicit assumptions, staged commitments, monitoring, and pre-defined remedies over claims of perfect foresight.
