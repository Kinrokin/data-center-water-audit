# Definitions used in the audit

## Withdrawal
Water removed from a source or delivered into a system. A withdrawal is not automatically equal to consumption.

## Consumption
Water not returned for immediate reuse in the relevant water environment during the accounting period. The exact definition depends on the source methodology.

## Delivery
Water supplied by a utility or other provider. Delivery is not automatically a direct measurement of evaporation or consumptive loss.

## Return / discharge
Liquid water leaving the facility or process and returned to a receiving system.

## Direct water
Water attributed to operation at the data-center site under the cited methodology.

## Electricity-related water
Water consumption attributed to the electricity used by the data center. This may occur far from the data-center building.

## Average footprint
An allocation of existing system impacts to a unit of electricity or service.

## Marginal / incremental impact
The additional effect caused by adding load. It can differ from an average footprint because the added demand may change dispatch, imports, infrastructure, or retirement decisions.

## Peak demand
The highest relevant demand over a defined interval. Annual totals can hide short periods that drive infrastructure constraints.

## Dependable capacity
The amount a supplying system can reliably provide under the planning conditions that matter, after existing commitments and operating constraints.

## Verified success
A task result shown to satisfy an independently defined requirement using evidence appropriate to the task. Agreement between two models is not, by itself, independent verification.
